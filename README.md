# ZambeziEstates

**Languages:** English | [Português](README.pt.md)

🌿 **Protecting wildlife, preserving nature.**

We work to protect Zambezi's wildlife and ecosystems through research, community engagement, and conservation initiatives.

* 🌍 **Website** → [zambeziestates.com](https://zambeziestates.com)
* ✉️ **Contact** → [info@zambeziestates.com](mailto:info@zambeziestates.com)

## What we work on

| area                     | what it is                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Research & science       | Wildlife and ecology research to guide evidence-based conservation         |
| Community empowerment   | Rural enterprise projects that support local communities as conservation partners |
| Environmental protection | Initiatives for clean rivers, sustainable energy, and ecosystem restoration |
| Sustainable funding     | Adventure sports and eco-tourism that help fund conservation               |

## Our conservation programs

| program                      | what we do                                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wildlife & ecology research  | Study local wildlife and ecosystems to inform conservation and protect biodiversity |
| Sustainable fuels research   | Explore fuel technologies that reduce environmental impact and support local energy independence |
| Clean & healthy rivers        | Protect river ecosystems through water-quality monitoring, pollution prevention, and community education |
| Rural enterprise projects    | Create economic opportunities while supporting conservation and environmental stewardship |
| Adventure sports for nature  | Raise funds and awareness for conservation through activities on land, water, and in the skies |

## Open tech we're exploring

Examples of open tools that could support field research and conservation work:

| project | what for |
| ------- | -------- |
| [QField](https://github.com/opengisch/QField) | Collect geospatial observations and map wildlife habitats, rivers, and project sites in the field |
| [ODK Collect](https://github.com/getodk/collect) | Gather survey and monitoring data with mobile forms, including when working offline |
| [iNaturalist for Android](https://github.com/inaturalist/iNaturalistAndroid) | Photograph and document species observations with help from a community of naturalists |
